ABOUT

Simple. Lightweight. ZClient.

ZClient is a custom Minecraft client created by Zizo. The project focuses on performance, customization, and creating a clean gameplay experience.

Built for Minecraft 26.2 with Fabric, ZClient is designed to work on both singleplayer and vanilla multiplayer servers.
